How this market works

Closed economy

Fifty agents trade a single good. Producers manufacture it each tick and post asks. Consumers earn wages, post bids, then eat. If they cannot consume their requirement, health falls; at zero they leave the market.

Price formation

The book matches highest bids to lowest asks. Trades print at the bid/ask midpoint, then policy (floor, ceiling, tax, subsidy) is applied. Unfilled imbalance nudges the quote by the volatility setting.

Traders & the printer

Healthy, well-capitalized consumers may stockpile and resell. One assigned agent can inject cash each tick, which typically lifts bids, inventories, and wealth inequality.
